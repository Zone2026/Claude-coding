Origin
From forest
to frequency

Every OTK begins as a single log selected by hand in the forests of Nagano Prefecture. A luthier's eye guides each cut — following the grain, preserving the character. What emerges is not a product, but an instrument.

The wood is air-dried for eighteen months before shaping begins. No kilns, no shortcuts. The result: a material acoustically inert in all the right places, and alive in all the right others.

About OTK Audio
We make things
that last

OTK Audio was founded in 2021 by a luthier and an acoustic engineer who shared a single conviction: that the materials a speaker is made from matter as much as its drivers. Based between Nagano and London, our workshop produces fewer than 500 speakers a year — by choice.

Repairability
Slow Design
We air-dry every log for eighteen months before a single cut is made. Good acoustics cannot be rushed. We don't pretend otherwise.
Honest Materials
No veneers. No composites. What you see is FSC-certified wood finished with food-grade linseed oil. That is everything on the outside.
Forever Repairable
Every OTK is designed to be opened, serviced, and upgraded. When a driver fails in ten years, we send you one. No questions, no charge.
